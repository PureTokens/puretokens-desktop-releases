# Pure Tokens Switch 0.3.0

Updates to client configuration, session recovery, and loading behavior. These notes describe the final behavior for this version; installer and real-client validation still follow the dual-device checklist.

- Improve failure-detail redaction for credentials, network addresses, host paths, and diagnostic bodies.

- Show action feedback in the upper-right corner of the window with multiline wrapping. Remove the repeated notice about automatically included required groups.

- Move usage troubleshooting guidance above the statistics cards and remove the redundant empty-records notice at the top.

- Remove the long hover hint from the Fast switch in normal states, retaining error feedback.
